Chiikawa Characters

Welcome to the world of Chiikawa! Here are some of the main characters:

Chiikawa

Chiikawa is an animal who is small and cute! They do weeding and other various jobs for money.

Hachiware

Hachiware is a cat with blue ears! They are Chiikawa's best friend and they live in a cave.

Usagi

Usagi is a very loud, yellow rabbit! Sometimes, they are obnoxious, but they are fun to be with. They also eat a lot.
